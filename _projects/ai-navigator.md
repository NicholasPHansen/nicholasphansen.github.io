AI-NAVIGATOR was a DTU research project developing an Advanced Navigation Assistance System that provides guidance to ship navigators in compliance with the IMO COLREGs ("Collision Regulations") and general good seamanship.

The project had two main work packages:

- **WP2**: codifying "good seamanship" from real-world AIS (Automatic Identification System) traffic data collected in Denmark, Norway, the USA, and Greece.
- **WP3**: COLREGs-compliant path/maneuver planners for both single-vessel and multi-vessel encounters, validated through integration with the SIMAC ship simulator (NMEA messages, radar, and AIS feeds).

Encounter detection between vessels was based on CPA/TCPA (Closest Point of Approach / Time to Closest Point of Approach) thresholds computed from AIS trajectories.

Team: Roberto Galeazzi, Dimitrios Papageorgiou, Jens Brauchli Jensen, Thomas Gulløv Longhi, Yaqub Prabowo.
