AI-NAVIGATOR is a DTU research project developing an Advanced Navigation Assistance System that provides guidance to ship navigators in compliance with the IMO COLREGs ("Collision Regulations") and general good seamanship. I've contributed to it since May 2023 as one of two postdoctoral researchers, investigating the formalization and learning of "good seamanship" from real-world vessel behavior.

A core output of the project is a large-scale AIS (Automatic Identification System) dataset of vessel interactions, codifying "good seamanship" from real-world traffic data collected in Denmark, Norway, the USA, and Greece -- supporting both current and future research in autonomous decision-making. The project also delivered COLREGs-compliant path/maneuver planners for single-vessel and multi-vessel encounters, validated through integration with the SIMAC ship simulator (NMEA messages, radar, and AIS feeds), with encounter detection based on CPA/TCPA (Closest Point of Approach / Time to Closest Point of Approach) thresholds.

Team: Roberto Galeazzi, Dimitrios Papageorgiou, Jens Brauchli Jensen, Thomas Gulløv Longhi, Yaqub Prabowo.
